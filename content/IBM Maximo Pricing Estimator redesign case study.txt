## Setup – The problem & context
Context: Maximo is a respected 40-year-old ALM software with many complex features that fit large-scale companies. To make it more flexible for companies to use it to manage their daily operations, Maximo uses a token currency called “AppPoints” as the main usage driver.


## What was broken or missing? - **Key insights**
My 5 interviews with sellers showed that:
Many users found the “AppPoints” term confusing. Even the internal sellers at IBM found it like that. It’s powerful but too complex.

*“They don’t make sense to us. Why are you doing this? Why is this so complicated?...It’s another level of abstraction… Instead of just saying the technician costs $20 amonth, it’s the technician is 2 AppPoints and 1 AppPoint equals $10.”*

And also, the pricing page (on IBM.com) doesn’t really articulate and break down well about what they’re paying for. Things are either too simple or too complex at the same time.

*[a picture of the current pricing page]* 

## Who was affected (users, business)?
Users don’t know what AppPoints is. My research shows that customers only care about how much they have to pay and what value they get.
Sellers having difficulties explaining what AppPoints is to users, and making quotes that converts AppPoints to real currency.
*“3 clients are like, we don’t like AppPoints.”*
*“Most of our customers are actually confused at just what an AppPoint is, much less the pricing calculator.”*


## What made this hard?
Complex and legacy process, too many people involved
Introducing a process disruption - as we already have a lot of tools. The problem is how to make it more efficient and controllable by the people using them. New tools doesn’t necessarily mean it’s better, we need a balance more.
Hard to explain the AppPoints concept to customers.
Internal sellers have to go through a rigid and manual quoting process.
